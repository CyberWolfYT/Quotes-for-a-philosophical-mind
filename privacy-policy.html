Privacy Policy

Quotes for a Philosophical Mind — Cyberwolf Studio
Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

This app allows members to voluntarily submit philosophical quotes and likes, which are stored in a Firebase Firestore database. No personal information such as names, email addresses, or location data is collected from general users.

How it is used

Submitted quotes and likes are displayed publicly within the app to all users. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Firebase

This app uses Google Firebase for data storage. Firebase may collect anonymous usage data as per Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

This app is rated 3+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
